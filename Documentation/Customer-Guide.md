**The complete journey, from first visit to a live company with mail arriving.**

This guide follows one customer end to end. Every screen you will meet is shown,
every button is explained, and every action says what happens next.

# Part 6 · Quotes and paying

Nothing is charged up front. We read your application, price it, and send you an
itemised quote. You pay only once you have seen the figure.

## 6.1 Billing & payments

**Billing & payments** in the sidebar is the whole money picture.

![Billing and payments](screenshots/06-01-billing-overview-desktop.png)

> Desktop: `Documentation/screenshots/06-01-billing-overview-desktop.png`
> Mobile: `Documentation/screenshots/06-01-billing-overview-mobile.png`

**The three figures** are **Amount due**, **Total paid**, and **Pending

**Quotes awaiting payment** is the list that matters. For each quote: the

> In these screenshots the payment history is empty. Settling a payment needs a

**Payment methods** shows what you can pay with:

- **USDT (TRC-20)** — active. USDT sent on the TRON network, confirmed on-chain

- **Pay by card** — marked **Soon**. Card payments are coming; there is nothing

## 6.3 Paying with USDT

Choosing USDT generates payment instructions for this quote only.

![Checkout — awaiting payment](screenshots/06-03-checkout-awaiting-desktop.png)

> Desktop: `Documentation/screenshots/06-03-checkout-awaiting-desktop.png`
> Mobile: `Documentation/screenshots/06-03-checkout-awaiting-mobile.png`

Work through it in this order:

1. **Read the amber warning first.** It names the network to send on and shows
   the **USDT token contract address**. Check the contract in your wallet
   matches. Funds sent on another network, or as a different token, cannot be
   recovered.
2. **Send the exact amount** shown under *Amount to send*. Use the copy button.
   The amount is how we match your transfer to this invoice, so a rounded or
   altered figure delays it.
3. **Send to the deposit address** shown, or scan the QR code with your wallet.
   Copy it with the button rather than typing it.
4. **Mind the timer.** *Send within 29:54* counts down the window in which this
   instruction is valid.

Once your transfer is on its way the page shows **Awaiting payment** and *Waiting
for your transfer* — it updates by itself as soon as we see the transfer
on-chain, so leave it open. You do not need to send us a hash or tell us you
paid.

**Cancel transfer** closes the window and returns you to billing. Your quote
stays open, so cancelling costs you nothing.

> The warning banner in these screenshots reads *TRON (TRC-20) Nile Testnet
> only* because the demo runs against TRON's test network. A live account names
> the TRON mainnet. **Always trust the network and contract address shown on
> your own screen**, not the one in a guide.

Once the payment confirms, the order moves to **Processing** and we begin filing.

# Part 7 · Your virtual mail room

A virtual mail room is a real street address that receives your post. We scan
everything that arrives and put it in your inbox, usually the same day. From
there you can read it, have it forwarded to you, or have it shredded.

## 7.1 Your rooms

**Virtual mail rooms** lists every room on your account with its address and
unread count.

![Virtual mail rooms](screenshots/07-01-mailroom-overview-desktop.png)

> Desktop: `Documentation/screenshots/07-01-mailroom-overview-desktop.png`
> Mobile: `Documentation/screenshots/07-01-mailroom-overview-mobile.png`

**Add new room** starts an ordinary order for the Virtual Mail Room service —
same three steps as [Part 4](#part-4--ordering-a-service).

## 7.2 A room's inbox

Opening a room shows its address and its post.

![Mail room inbox](screenshots/07-02-mailroom-inbox-desktop.png)

> Desktop: `Documentation/screenshots/07-02-mailroom-inbox-desktop.png`
> Mobile: `Documentation/screenshots/07-02-mailroom-inbox-mobile.png`

**The three figures** are **New mail**, **Pending requests** (forwarding or
shredding we are working on), and **Total items**.

**Three tabs** split the room: **Inbox** (current post), **Requests** (your
forwarding and shredding requests and where they have got to), and **History**
(everything handled). **Search by sender…** and the **All statuses** filter
narrow the list.

Each item shows a thumbnail of the scan, the **sender**, the note our mail team
added, when it was **received**, when its **storage expires**, and its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Scanned, not opened yet. |
| **Action requested** | We think this one needs a decision from you — its button reads **Respond** rather than **View**. |

> **Storage expires** is a real date. We hold the physical item until then; after
> that it is disposed of securely. The scan stays in your account. If you want
> the original, request forwarding before that date.

## 7.3 Reading an item

**View** (or **Respond**) opens the item in a panel over the list — a side panel
on desktop, a full screen on a phone.

![Mail item](screenshots/07-03-mail-item-viewer-desktop.png)

> Desktop: `Documentation/screenshots/07-03-mail-item-viewer-desktop.png`
> Mobile: `Documentation/screenshots/07-03-mail-item-viewer-mobile.png`

The header shows the sender, the date received, and which room it came to. **‹ 1
of 3 ›** steps through the room without going back to the list. **Esc** or **✕**
closes the panel.

- **Preview document** opens the scan in a new browser tab, through a
  short-lived secure link. If the tab does not appear, allow pop-ups for the
  site.
- **Download PDF** saves the scan.
- **Request forwarding** — we post the physical item to your company address.
- **Request shredding** — we destroy the physical item securely. The scan stays
  in your account.

## 7.4 Requesting forwarding or shredding

Choose either action and the panel confirms with **We're working on your request

When we finish a service, the result becomes a permanent **record** in your

## 9.2 A company record

![Company record](screenshots/09-02-company-record-desktop.png)

> Desktop: `Documentation/screenshots/09-02-company-record-desktop.png`
> Mobile: `Documentation/screenshots/09-02-company-record-mobile.png`

The heading is the registered name, with its record number, the service that
produced it, a link back to the **order**, and the delivery date. Below that:

- **Registration** — registered name (exactly as filed), registration number,
  status (**Active**, pending, dissolved), formation date, and jurisdiction.
- **Tax** — your **EIN / Tax ID** as issued by the tax authority.

> **Next annual report due** is the date to diary. We hold the registered agent

## 9.3 Asking for follow-up work

**What would you like to do?** offers the after-sales work available on this
record, each with its usual turnaround:

| Request | What it does | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Request a certified copy** | A stamped copy of your formation documents. | 3–5 business days |
| **File an amendment** | Change the company name, address, or members. | 5–10 business days |
| **File my annual report** | We prepare and file it with the registry for you. | Within 5 business days |

Choosing one opens a short dialog: it repeats what the request is and how long it
takes, and gives you an optional box — *Anything else we should know?* — for
details such as how many copies you need or exactly what is changing.

**Submit request** sends it to our team; **Cancel** closes without sending. If
the work carries a fee, you get a quote for it the same way as any other order —
review it and pay from **Billing & payments**.

*Internal. Remove before publishing.*

**Environment.** Captured from the local development stack (frontend
`localhost:5173`, API `localhost:4000`) on 30 July 2026, against a demo customer
`daniel.reyes.demo@martyglobal.test`. Three orders exist: `ORD-2026-VFY4BTSX`

**Capture settings.** Desktop 1440 × 900, mobile 390 × 844, both at 2× pixel
density. The portal and admin shells are full-height with an inner scroll area,
so `fullPage` captures nothing beyond the viewport; long screens were captured by
growing the viewport to the measured content height instead. Product surfaces
render at 80% root font size (the compact-density scheme), which is why portal
text looks smaller relative to marketing pages.

**States not captured, and why.**

1. **A settled payment, payment history, and an invoice.** Crediting a quote
   requires a real USDT transfer on TRON, matched on-chain by the reconciliation
   job. No transfer was made, so *Payment history* is empty everywhere it
   appears and no invoice download exists. To complete these, send a Nile
   testnet USDT transfer of the exact quoted amount to the deposit address on
   the checkout screen, then recapture: `06-01`, plus new shots of a paid order
   and an invoice.
2. **Card payment.** Deferred by design; the option renders disabled with a
   **Soon** tag. No screenshot to add until it ships.
3. **An in-app scan viewer.** **Preview document** opens the scan in a new tab
   via a presigned R2 URL, so there is no in-app preview state to photograph.
   The guide describes the new-tab behaviour instead.
4. **Empty states.** Every screen was captured populated. The empty variants
   (*No orders yet*, *You're all caught up*, *No payments yet*) exist and read
   well — worth a short "your first week" section if you want them.
